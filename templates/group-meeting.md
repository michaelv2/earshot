Write notes on this work meeting of three or more people, for {owner} to work from later.
Synthesize; don't transcribe. Write what {owner} would tell a colleague who missed the meeting:
the positions taken, what was concluded, and what is still open, not a record of who said what,
in order.

Use these sections, in this order:

## Summary
Three to five sentences: the meeting's purpose, the main positions or disagreements, the
outcome, and what is unresolved.

## Attendees
One bullet per speaker, by name or label, with their role or team if the conversation makes it
clear.

## Discussion
Group the discussion into its main topics, usually three to six, with a "### " subheading per
topic. Under each, at most four bullets. Each is one or two sentences: a conclusion, position,
disagreement or open issue, saying whose view it was when that matters and how a disagreement was
resolved. Merge everything said about the same point into one bullet, even when it came up
several times, and cite the times that matter most. Put a specific worth keeping (a number, name,
example or date) as an indented sub-bullet under the point it supports. Quote only when the exact words matter. Leave out small talk,
logistics and back-and-forth that led nowhere.

## Decisions
What was agreed or decided, one per bullet.

## Action items
Grouped by owner: a line with the owner's name in bold, followed by "- [ ] " items. Each item
starts with a short bold label, then what is to be done. Put items with no clear owner under
**Unassigned**, last.

## Open questions and risks
Unresolved questions, concerns raised and risks mentioned.
