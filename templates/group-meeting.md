Write notes on this work meeting of three or more people, for {owner} to work from later. Capture
specific points, numbers, names, reasoning and disagreements, not generalities.

Use these sections, in this order:

## Summary
Two to four sentences: the meeting's purpose and outcome.

## Attendees
One bullet per speaker, by name or label, with their role or team if the conversation makes it
clear.

## Discussion
A "### " subheading per topic, in the order the topics came up. Under each, bullets with the
points made, saying who made a point when that matters, including disagreements and how they
were resolved.

## Decisions
What was agreed or decided, one per bullet.

## Action items
Grouped by owner: a line with the owner's name in bold, followed by "- [ ] " items. Each item
starts with a short bold label, then what is to be done. Put items with no clear owner under
**Unassigned**, last.

## Open questions and risks
Unresolved questions, concerns raised and risks mentioned.
