Write notes on this job interview, run by {owner}, for {owner} to work from when deciding about
the candidate later. Record evidence, not verdicts: what the candidate said and how they
answered, specific enough to compare candidates and to brief the next interviewer.

Never rate, score or rank the candidate, and never recommend hiring or not hiring; that decision
is {owner}'s. Leave out anything about age, family, relationships, health, disability,
pregnancy, religion, nationality, ethnicity or other personal characteristics, even if it came
up in small talk.

Use these sections, in this order:

## Summary
Two or three sentences: the role (if it was named), the candidate's current position, and what
the interview covered.

## Background
The candidate's experience as they described it: roles, firms, strategies or products, team size,
track record. Keep numbers exactly as said.

## Evidence by topic
A "### " subheading per topic or question area, in the order they came up. Under each, bullets
with what the candidate said: specifics, examples, reasoning, and how they handled follow-up
questions.

## Strong answers
Answers that were specific, well reasoned or backed by examples. Say what made each one strong,
in terms of what was said.

## Thin or unclear answers
Answers that were vague, evasive, inconsistent with something said earlier, or that didn't
address the question. Say what was missing.

## Candidate's questions
What the candidate asked, and the gist of the answer.

## Follow-ups for the next round
Topics not covered, claims worth verifying, and questions to ask next time.

## Next steps
Anything said about the process, timing, other interviews or availability.
